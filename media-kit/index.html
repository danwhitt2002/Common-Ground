About

Hey, I'm Dan

I'm Dan, a UGC creator based in Rio de Janeiro, creating for hospitality and food & beverage brands — including Naveia, Brazil's award-winning oat milk brand. My signature format is street interviews: stopping real people on the streets of Rio for raw, unfiltered reactions and opinions on camera. For brands further afield with a Brazilian story to tell, I bring that same energy: content shot on the ground in Rio, not stock footage.

Prefer more control? I can also run guided street interviews with prompted questions to hit specific talking points.
